    What this is, and what it is not

    Every other rank in this app is one it made up: the practice ladder unlocks on a streak it chose, the anzan rungs are its own, the village rank is fiction. Soroban has had an external standard for a century — the 珠算検定 grading exam — and this page is modelled on it: the term counts, the digit widths, the grade at which minus terms and division appear, 100 points a section and 70 to pass.

    It certifies nothing. This is not the Japan Chamber of Commerce & Industry, the papers here are shorter than the real ones (five questions a section, with the clock scaled to match), and the division is exact where a real paper above about 4級 carries remainders. Passing 3級 in a browser is not 3級. What does transfer is the shape of the work and the standard you are held to.
