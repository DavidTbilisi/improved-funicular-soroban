Place-Value Peg Soroban

Timed recall drills for the mnemonic number-codec — pegs, cells, scenes, seals — plus the finger times-table track.

Drill mode — timed against the METER pass-floors

Pick a deck; items keep coming until you stop. Typed decks check exactly (case-insensitive, 0x optional); recall decks reveal for self-grading — the timer measures until you hit Reveal. A rep passes its floor only if it's correct and under time. Session stats persist per deck (localStorage). Drill order per the tier discipline: atomics (pegs, faces, actions) → cells → scenes → seals. The finger decks are their own track, a ladder over the hard 6–9 corner of the times table: Hands → product teaches you to read a pair of hands fluently (Fig. 1), Finger × then drills the bare facts until recall displaces the hands — its floor is set for recall, so your under-floor share is your automaticity — and 9-fold × covers the 9s row (Fig. 2). These decks deal every fact once per round, re-deal a miss two items later (with the method redrawn), and deal facts your record shows as weak twice.
