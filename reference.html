Cube digit faces (vertical axis)

Each digit has exactly one form, mirroring the bead grammar: 0 = bare column, 1–4 = earth face, 5 = 🌹 Rose alone (the sky bead), 6–9 = Rose + earth face. Every image wears its face color. 😇 Angel (white, die-face 6) is retired from digit duty — reserved-icon inventory. Orientation carries no digit information. 16×16 hex extension pegs (A–F, frozen 2026-07-02)

Audio A–F = NATO phonetic (rhyme can't extend to letters — B/C/D/E all rhyme "-ee"); visual A–F = letterform objects (same logic as donut=0, wand=1). Mnemonic: "Radio says the tens, shape shows the units." Guards: Alpha wolf is gray + howling vs Foxtrot fox orange + dancing; the Moon glows (the croissant is baked); the Flag is the generic red one. The radix lives in the mode, never in the image — cell 47 is decimal 47 or 0x47 depending on the consumer.

A–Z food peg columns

Integer columns use A–K upright; decimal columns reuse A–D flipped. The flip is the only difference between a place and its fractional echo.
