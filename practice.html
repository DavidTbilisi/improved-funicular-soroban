Guided practice — leveled bead drills

Work the ladder: pick a level, then solve problems on the beads with the keyboard. The gate is automaticity — a solve only counts if it's under the level's time floor and fumble-free (an illegal move or a reset spoils it). A slow or fumbled solve resets your streak, so you must string together several fluent solves in a row to unlock the next level. 🔒 = locked · progress is saved per browser.

Theory — the two complements

Every bead move beyond the direct ones is one of two exchanges. When the earth beads run out but the digit stays under ten, trade through five: +d = +5 −(5−d). When the digit would cross ten, trade through the next rod: +d = +10 −(10−d) (and the mirror images for subtraction).
