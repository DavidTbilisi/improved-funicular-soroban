Guided practice — leveled bead drills

Work the ladder: pick a level, then solve problems on the beads above with the keyboard. The gate is automaticity — a solve only counts if it's under the level's time floor and fumble-free (an illegal move or a reset spoils it). A slow or fumbled solve resets your streak, so you must string together several fluent solves in a row to unlock the next level. 🔒 = locked · progress is saved per browser.
