Place-Value Peg Soroban

Every column is a food. Upright = integer place, flipped = decimal place. The food stays put; the decimal point is the mirror.

Set a number

Click any bead to move it. Each column has 1 sky bead (worth 5) and 4 earth beads (worth 1 each). Integer places sit left of the green point with upright pegs (Apple = ones, Banana = tens…). Decimal places sit right of the point with flipped pegs reusing the same foods (flipped Apple = tenths, flipped Banana = hundredths…).

A–Z food peg columns

Integer columns use A–K upright; decimal columns reuse A–D flipped. The flip is the only difference between a place and its fractional echo.
