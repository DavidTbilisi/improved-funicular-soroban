Multiplication & division — the real soroban method

The authentic rod-placement algorithms from the Japan Chamber of Commerce Soroban handbook. Pick a type; the beads are seeded with the multiplier / multiplicand (or dividend / divisor). Then work the highlighted rods with the keyboard, one step at a time — each partial product / subtraction lands on a fixed rod, and the trainer advances only when the board reads the right value, so it verifies your rod placement, not just the final answer. Stuck on a step? Do this step plays it for you.

The multiplication table

Every step of soroban multiplication and division reads exactly one cell of this table — multiplication adds it onto the board, division subtracts it back off. While a problem runs, the cell the current step reads is marked in red. The handbook's advice stands: know the table cold and the rods do the rest.
