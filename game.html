How the village works

Every soroban point (sp) is minted by your own bead work: take a contract, the beads are seeded to its start value, and reaching the answer on the board is the solve — the keyboard's move-legality means a correct final value proves correct technique. A fumble-free solve earns the clean bonus, beating the contract's pace floor earns the speed bonus, and every clean solve already on your 🔥 streak adds to the payout (the step scales with the contract; up to 10 banked solves count) — one fumble breaks it. Harder arithmetic simply pays more, per solve and per second.

Spend sp on buildings (click a palette button, then an empty plot); each solved contract also advances the village one day, and every building pays its daily yield. Click a placed building to upgrade it — the resource cost is held up front and the level is earned by solving a harder contract. Levels never cap: yields scale with level forever and the cost grows with it. A level-3 shrine founds the village; after the goal ladder, the 💡 hint keeps pointing at the cheapest next upgrade. When the stores overflow, 🏮 light a festival: every payout is ×1.5 for the next 10 solves. The feast is priced to the village — ten days of its own production, never less than 150 🌾 + 50 🪵 + 100 🪙 — so turning grain and coin back into sp stays a real decision however rich the village grows.
