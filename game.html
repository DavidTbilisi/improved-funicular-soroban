How the village works

Every soroban point (sp) is minted by your own bead work: take a contract, the beads are seeded to its start value, and reaching the answer on the board is the solve — the keyboard's move-legality means a correct final value proves correct technique. A fumble-free solve earns the clean bonus, beating the contract's pace floor earns the speed bonus, and every clean solve already on your 🔥 streak adds +1 sp (up to +10) — one fumble breaks it. Harder arithmetic simply pays more.

Spend sp on buildings (click a palette button, then an empty plot); each solved contract also advances the village one day, and every building pays its daily yield. Click a placed building to upgrade it — the resource cost is held up front and the level is earned by solving a harder contract. A level-3 shrine founds the village.
