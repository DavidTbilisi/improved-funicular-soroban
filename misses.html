Place-Value Peg Soroban

間違いノート — the mistake book: every question you got wrong, until you get it right twice.

Work the book

    Working the things you got wrong is the highest-yield habit there is after spacing, and until now this app threw them all away: a drill miss was re-dealt inside its own round and forgotten, a wrong line on a kyu paper appeared once on the mark sheet and went with it, a lost anzan round showed its terms back and moved on. Now every page that can grade an answer writes its misses here, in one shape.
    The same miss is one entry, with a count — missing something twice is not two problems. Two rights in a row take it out of the book, because one right is a coin flip on a two-choice answer and a fluke on most of the rest; a later miss puts the progress back to zero. A wrong answer shows you the right one immediately: this is not a test, the entry is already a known miss, and hiding the answer a second time teaches nothing. The book is capped at the most recent 80 — a book you cannot finish is a book nobody opens.
